Every path

Every message type, SysEx up to 10 KB, 2000 notes at once and a clock, both ways. About half a minute.

04by hand

At the board

  • panic Press it when asked.
  • a trs b Flip it when asked.
  • midi in Play a keyboard on it.
  • midi out A synth plays nine notes.

Never plug midi out into midi in. H2H passes midi in on to midi out, so the messages go round forever.

Update without this page

Hold the BOOT button of one side while you plug in its USB cable. A drive named RPI-RP2 opens. Drag the firmware file onto it, and the side restarts with it. The second file is only for a blue side whose role is fixed in the firmware.
